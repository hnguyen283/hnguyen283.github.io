LLM Council

AI research orchestrator · llm.welllifeapp.com · Role: creator & architect

LLM Council runs a panel of AI models against a research question and adjudicates the result. Gemini collects evidence through grounded search; GPT analyses it, debates the weakest claims and scores every source; a deterministic judge written in Java turns those scores into source tiers and an overall confidence band before a short, direct answer is written. The aim is AI answers people can trust, without depending on a single model, vendor or prompt.

How a research run works

  1. Planlocal AI
  2. CollectGemini
  3. Validate3+ domains
  4. AnalyseGPT
  5. DebateGPT
  6. Score0–100 per source
  7. JudgeJava
  8. AnswerGPT
  • Multi-model research panel
  • Own architecture
  • Source scoring
  • Confidence bands
  • English & Vietnamese
  • Live product
